LOSS/RESET CONTRACT
The opt-in upstream guard instantiates agents without moving them, reads and
validates robot TF before each move, and uses that SAME transform to update the
robot. Missing initial TF waits. After a valid robot has been seen, missing,
older-than0.5s, future TF or ROS rollback latches a pre-integration stop until
simulator restart. This checks each step, not asynchronous/preemptive braking:
a step already begun with fresh validated input may finish before later loss.
No pure sleep/post-hoc pause race is used. start_paused and require_fresh_robot
must both be true; do not bypass the guard through another simulator binary.
Bridge likewise latches after dependency loss and suppresses output; planner
expires last real observation and STOPs. Restart BOTH simulator and bridge to
recover/reset; restore fresh user state, then unpause. New navigation goals do
not clear the guard. In blank editor mode new goals may reset the people only.
Missed interactions cannot be reconstructed by fresh stamping.
